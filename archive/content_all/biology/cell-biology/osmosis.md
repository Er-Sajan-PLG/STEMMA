  The net movement of water molecules across a selectively permeable membrane from a

common_misconceptions:
  - Osmosis moves solute particles through the membrane (osmosis moves the solvent — water — while solutes stay behind).
  - Osmosis and diffusion are identical (osmosis is a special case of diffusion — the diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane).
  - Water moves toward where there is more water (water moves toward the higher solute concentration to dilute it).

## Notes

Osmosis is diffusion applied to water across a membrane: it is central to cell water
balance, plant support (turgor), and kidney function.
