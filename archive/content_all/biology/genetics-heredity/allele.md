definition: >-
  One of the alternative forms of a gene that occupy the same locus on homologous chromosomes and can
  specify alternative versions of a trait; a diploid organism carries two alleles for each autosomal gene,
  which may be identical (homozygous) or different (heterozygous).

  - "Mendel's breeding experiments with pea plants demonstrated that alleles segregate and assort independently across generations."
common_misconceptions:
  - Dominant alleles are always the most frequent in a population (dominance describes expression, not frequency).
  - An individual can have more than two alleles of the same autosomal gene (an individual has two; a population may harbour many alleles).

## Notes

Alleles are the units whose segregation and independent assortment produce Mendelian inheritance patterns.
