  The recurring ways in which traits and alleles are transmitted from parents to offspring, including

  - "Mendel's controlled crosses established the laws of segregation and independent assortment underlying most patterns."
common_misconceptions:
  - A dominant phenotype means the trait is necessarily common in the population (dominance concerns expression, not frequency).
  - All traits follow simple dominant-recessive rules (many are codominant, incompletely dominant, or polygenic).

  - Recognize patterns from pedigrees to infer mode of inheritance.

## Notes

Inheritance patterns translate allele combinations and gene location into the observable transmission of
traits across generations.
