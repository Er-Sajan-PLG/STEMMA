  The biological process by which autotrophic organisms (such as plants and algae) convert light energy
  into stored chemical energy in glucose, using carbon dioxide and water while releasing oxygen as a byproduct.

common_misconceptions:
  - Photosynthesis occurs to produce energy for plants instead of storing energy as carbohydrates.
  - Photosynthesis occurs during the day while cellular respiration only occurs at night (respiration occurs continuously).
  - Plants obtain their primary mass from soil nutrients rather than carbon dioxide gas from air.

## Notes

Photosynthesis occurs in chloroplasts, transferring light energy into chemical bonds.
