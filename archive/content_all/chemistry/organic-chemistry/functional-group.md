name: Functional Group
domain: chemistry
status: draft
definition: >-
  A specific atom or group of atoms within an organic molecule that determines the characteristic
  chemical reactions and properties of that molecule. The same functional group confers predictable
  behaviour regardless of the carbon skeleton to which it is attached.
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "The hydroxyl group (—OH) makes a compound an alcohol; the carboxyl group (—COOH) makes it a carboxylic acid."
  - "A carbonyl group (C=O) characterizes aldehydes and ketones."
key_experiments:
  - "Use chemical tests (such as sodium for alcohols or sodium carbonate for acids) to identify functional groups."
common_misconceptions:
  - The carbon skeleton alone determines a compound's chemistry (the functional group dominates reactivity).
  - Every functional group reacts the same way in every molecule (reactivity is modulated by the rest of the molecule).
learning_objectives:
  - Recognize common functional groups from their structural formulas.
  - Predict chemical properties from the functional groups present.
  - Use functional groups to name and classify organic compounds.
real_world_applications:
  - Drug design relies on functional groups that bind biological targets.
  - Functional groups define the behaviour of dyes, solvents, and agrochemicals.
