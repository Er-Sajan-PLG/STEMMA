type: concept
name: Percentage
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  A ratio or fraction expressed as parts per hundred, denoted with the % symbol.
  x% means x/100 or x hundredths. Percentages provide a standardized way to compare
  proportions regardless of the whole's size.
symbol: "%"
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "50% = 50/100 = 1/2 = 0.5"
  - "25% discount means paying 75% of original price"
  - "Interest rate of 5% per annum"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Percentages above 100% are impossible (150% means 1.5 times the original)"
  - "A 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease returns to original (it gives 75% of original)"
  - "Percentage points and percent are the same (going from 10% to 15% is a 5 percentage-point increase, but a 50% increase)"
learning_objectives:
  - Convert between percentages, fractions, and decimals
  - Calculate percentage of a quantity
  - Calculate percentage increase/decrease
  - Solve problems involving profit, loss, discount, tax, and interest
  - Distinguish between percentage change and percentage points
real_world_applications:
  - "Finance: interest rates, discounts, tax, profit margins"
  - "Statistics: survey results, population changes"
  - "Science: concentration, efficiency, error margins"
key_experiments:
  - "Grid paper: shade squares to visualize percentages as parts per hundred"
