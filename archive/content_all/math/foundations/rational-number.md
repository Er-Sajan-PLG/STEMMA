name: Rational Number
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  Any number that can be expressed as the quotient p/q of two integers, where q ≠ 0.
  Rational numbers include all integers, fractions, terminating decimals, and repeating decimals.
symbol: ℚ
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "1/2 = 0.5 (terminating decimal)"
  - "1/3 = 0.333... (repeating decimal)"
  - "-4/5 = -0.8"
  - "7 = 7/1 (every integer is rational)"
common_misconceptions:
  - "All decimals are rational (non-terminating, non-repeating decimals like π are irrational)"
  - "Fractions with large denominators are not rational (any integer ratio is rational)"
  - "0.999... ≠ 1 (it equals 1 exactly)"
learning_objectives:
  - Identify rational numbers in fraction, decimal, and percentage forms
  - Convert between fraction, decimal, and percentage representations
  - Perform arithmetic operations with rational numbers
  - "Understand density: between any two rationals there is another rational"
real_world_applications:
  - Measurement with fractions (cooking, construction)
  - Financial calculations (interest rates, discounts)
  - Probability (ratios of favorable to total outcomes)
key_experiments:
  - "Long division exploration: discover which fractions terminate and which repeat"
