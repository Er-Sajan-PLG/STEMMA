id: stemma:math.trigonometric-ratio
type: concept
name: Trigonometric Ratio
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  Ratios of sides in a right triangle relative to an acute angle. The three primary ratios:
  sine (opposite/hypotenuse), cosine (adjacent/hypotenuse), tangent (opposite/adjacent).
  Extended to reciprocal ratios: cosecant, secant, cotangent.
symbol: sin θ, cos θ, tan θ
unit: null
equation: sin θ = opp/hyp  |  cos θ = adj/hyp  |  tan θ = opp/adj
examples:
  - "30°: sin = 1/2, cos = √3/2, tan = 1/√3"
  - "45°: sin = cos = 1/√2, tan = 1"
  - "60°: sin = √3/2, cos = 1/2, tan = √3"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Trig ratios work for any triangle (only right triangles, or use laws of sines/cosines)"
  - "sin θ = 1 means θ = 1° (sin 90° = 1, sin θ ≤ 1 always)"
  - "cos θ is 'cosine of θ' not 'cos × θ'"
learning_objectives:
  - Define sin, cos, tan for acute angles
  - Use standard angle values (0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°)
  - Solve right triangles (find missing sides/angles)
  - Apply to angles of elevation and depression
real_world_applications:
  - Surveying (height of buildings, mountains)
  - Navigation (bearings, course)
  - Physics (vector components, forces)
  - Engineering (stress analysis)
key_experiments:
  - "Clinometer: measure angle to top of tree/building, calculate height"
