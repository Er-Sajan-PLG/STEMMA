id: stemma:math.area
type: quantity
name: Area
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The measure of the size of a two-dimensional surface or region. Measured in square units.
  The amount of space enclosed within a boundary.
symbol: A
unit: square metre (m²), square centimetre (cm²), hectare (ha)
equation: "Rectangle: A = l × w  |  Triangle: A = ½ × b × h  |  Circle: A = πr²"
examples:
  - "Room floor: 5m × 4m = 20 m²"
  - "Triangle base 10cm, height 6cm → area = 30 cm²"
  - "Field: 1 hectare = 10,000 m²"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Area and perimeter are the same (perimeter is boundary length, area is enclosed space)"
  - "Larger perimeter always means larger area (a long thin rectangle can have large perimeter but small area)"
  - "Area units don't need to be squared (they must: cm², m², km²)"
learning_objectives:
  - Calculate area of rectangles, triangles, parallelograms, trapezoids, circles
  - Find area of composite shapes by decomposition
  - Convert between area units (1 m² = 10,000 cm²)
  - Solve real-world problems (flooring, painting, land area)
real_world_applications:
  - Construction (flooring, roofing, painting)
  - Agriculture (field area, seeding rates)
  - Geography (country/region area)
  - Physics (pressure = force/area)
key_experiments:
  - "Grid paper: count squares to find area of irregular shapes"
