name: Volume
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The measure of the three-dimensional space occupied by an object or enclosed by a surface.
  Measured in cubic units. Capacity refers to how much a container can hold.
symbol: V
unit: cubic metre (m³), cubic centimetre (cm³), litre (L = 1000 cm³)
equation: "Cuboid: V = l × w × h  |  Cylinder: V = πr²h  |  Sphere: V = ⁴/₃πr³"
examples:
  - "Box: 2m × 3m × 4m = 24 m³"
  - "Water bottle: 500 mL = 500 cm³"
  - "Sphere radius 3cm → volume ≈ 113.1 cm³"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Volume and capacity are the same (volume is space occupied, capacity is how much a container holds)"
  - "Doubling dimensions doubles volume (it multiplies volume by 8)"
  - "1 L = 1 m³ (1 L = 0.001 m³ = 1000 cm³)"
learning_objectives:
  - Calculate volume of cuboids, cylinders, cones, spheres, pyramids
  - Convert between volume units (cm³, m³, L, mL)
  - Solve problems involving filling/emptying containers
  - Understand relationship between volume and capacity
real_world_applications:
  - Packaging and shipping
  - Medicine dosage (mL)
  - Fuel tank capacity
  - Construction (concrete volume)
key_experiments:
  - "Water displacement: find volume of irregular objects"
