type: quantity
name: Median
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The middle value in an ordered data set. If there are two middle values, the median
  is their average. It divides the data into two equal halves and is robust against outliers.
symbol: M, x̃
unit: same as data units
equation: null
examples:
  - "Data: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 → median = 30"
  - "Data: 10, 20, 30, 40 → median = (20+30)/2 = 25"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Median is the same as mean (they differ when data is skewed)"
  - "Median requires the data to be numerical (ordinal data also works)"
  - "Median is affected by outliers (it is resistant to outliers)"
learning_objectives:
  - Find median for odd and even number of data points
  - Find median from frequency tables
  - Compare median vs mean for skewed distributions
  - Understand median as 50th percentile
real_world_applications:
  - Household income (median preferred over mean)
  - Median home prices
  - Survival time in medical studies
key_experiments:
  - "Human median: line up by height, find middle person"
