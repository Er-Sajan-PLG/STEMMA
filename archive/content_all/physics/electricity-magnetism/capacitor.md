name: Capacitance
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: The ability of a system of conductors separated by an insulator to store electric
  charge, measured as the ratio of stored charge to the potential difference across the system.
  A capacitor is the device realizing this property.
symbol: C
unit: farad (F)
equation: C = Q/V
examples:
  - "A parallel-plate capacitor stores charge proportional to the voltage across its plates."
  - "In a camera flash, a capacitor discharges rapidly to deliver a bright pulse of light."
key_experiments:
  - "Charge a capacitor through a resistor and record the exponential growth of voltage."
common_misconceptions:
  - "Capacitance depends on the geometry and dielectric, not on the charge or voltage applied."
  - "A capacitor in a DC circuit blocks steady current but stores and releases charge."

  - "Compute capacitance and stored charge for parallel-plate geometry."
  - "Describe how a dielectric increases capacitance."

  - "Energy storage in battery-free memory and camera flashes."
  - "Filtering and smoothing in power-supply circuits."
