name: Density
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The mass per unit volume of a substance. Density determines whether objects float or sink
  in fluids.
symbol: ρ (rho)
unit: kilogram per cubic metre (kg/m³)
equation: ρ = m / V
examples:
  - "Oil floats on water because it is less dense."
  - "A full and an empty box have the same volume but different densities."
common_misconceptions:
  - Heavy objects always sink (it depends on density, not just mass).
  - Density is the same as weight (density is mass/volume; weight is a force).
  - All solids are denser than all liquids (ice floats on water — ice is less dense).
learning_objectives:
  - Define density as mass per unit volume.
  - Calculate density from mass and volume.
  - Explain floating and sinking in terms of density.
  - Measure density of regular and irregular objects.
real_world_applications:
  - Ship design (steel ships float because they displace enough water).
  - Hot air balloons (less dense air rises).
  - Oil spills (oil floats on water).
  - Material identification.
key_experiments:
  - "Measuring density of a regular solid and an irregular solid using displacement."
