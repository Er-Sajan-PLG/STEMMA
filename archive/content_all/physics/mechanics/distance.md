  The total length of the path traveled between two positions, regardless of direction. Distance is
  a scalar quantity — it has magnitude but no direction.

  - "Reading the odometer of a car — total path length travelled."

  - "Measure a curved path with a string, then compare with a straight ruler line for the same route."
common_misconceptions:
  - Distance and displacement are always the same (they differ whenever the path is not a straight line).
  - Distance can be negative (distance is always non-negative).

  - Define distance as a scalar quantity.
  - Distinguish between distance and displacement.

## Notes

Distance is the most fundamental spatial measurement. It is the foundation for defining speed, velocity, and the geometry of motion. All motion analysis begins with position and distance.
