name: Wave Speed
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The speed at which a wave propagates through a medium. Wave speed depends on the properties
  of the medium, not on frequency or amplitude.
symbol: v
unit: metre per second (m/s)
equation: v = f·λ
examples:
  - "Sound travels at about 343 m/s in air; light at 3×10⁸ m/s."
  - "Deep-ocean tsunami waves can travel at hundreds of km/h."
key_experiments:
  - "Ripple tank: measure frequency and wavelength and verify v = f·λ."
common_misconceptions:
  - Higher frequency waves always travel faster (in the same medium, all waves of the same type travel at the same speed).
  - Wave speed is infinite (all waves have finite speed — light travels at 3×10⁸ m/s in vacuum).
learning_objectives:
  - Calculate wave speed from frequency and wavelength.
  - Explain why wave speed depends on the medium.
  - Compare wave speeds in different media.
real_world_applications:
  - Thunder and lightning (light travels much faster than sound).
  - Echos and sonar.
  - Seismic wave analysis.
