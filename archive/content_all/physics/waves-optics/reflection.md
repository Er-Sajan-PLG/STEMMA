name: Reflection of Light
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: The bouncing back of light from a surface. The law of reflection states
  that the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection.
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: θ_i = θ_r
examples:
- Your image in a plane mirror; sunlight glinting off a lake.
- Retroreflectors on bicycles return light to its source.
key_experiments:
- 'Ray box at a plane mirror: measure and show angle of incidence = angle of reflection.'
- Verifying the law of reflection using a plane mirror and ray box.
common_misconceptions:
- Reflection only happens from mirrors (all surfaces reflect some light).
- The angle of incidence is measured from the surface (it is measured from the normal).
- Rough surfaces do not reflect (they reflect diffusely).
learning_objectives:
- State the law of reflection.
- Draw ray diagrams for reflection from plane mirrors.
- Distinguish between regular and diffuse reflection.
- Explain how images are formed in plane mirrors.
real_world_applications:
- Mirrors.
- Periscopes.
- Rear-view mirrors.
- Optical instruments.
