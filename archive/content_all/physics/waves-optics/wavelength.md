name: Wavelength
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The distance between two consecutive points in phase on a wave — for example, crest to crest
  or trough to trough. Wavelength determines the character of the wave (color of light, pitch of sound).
symbol: λ (lambda)
unit: metre (m)
equation: null
real_world_applications:
  - "The colour of light corresponds to its wavelength."
  - "Antennas are sized to the wavelength they carry; X-ray wavelengths image bones."
examples:
  - "Red light has a longer wavelength than blue light."
  - "Longer radio waves bend around hills more easily than short ones."
key_experiments:
  - "Ripple tank: measure the wavelength of periodic waves and change the wave generator frequency."
common_misconceptions:
  - Wavelength is the height of a wave (height is amplitude; wavelength is distance between cycles).
  - All waves have the same wavelength (wavelength varies enormously — from kilometers to nanometers).
learning_objectives:
  - Define wavelength and identify it on a wave diagram.
  - Relate wavelength to frequency and wave speed.
  - Compare wavelengths of different types of waves.
