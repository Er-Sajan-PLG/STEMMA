  The epistemic distinction between direct empirical data gathered through sensing/measurement (observation)
  and the logical explanation or conclusion derived from those data based on prior knowledge (inference).

  - Inferences are facts (inferences are interpretations that require logical justification and testing).
  - Scientific theories are just guesses (scientific theories are well-supported explanatory frameworks based on extensive observations).

  - Distinguish direct observations from logical inferences in scientific reports.

  - Evaluate the strength of evidence supporting a scientific inference.

## Notes

Distinguishing observation from inference is fundamental to scientific literacy and critical thinking.
