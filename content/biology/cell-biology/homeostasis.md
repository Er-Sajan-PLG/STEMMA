  The maintenance of a stable internal environment (temperature, pH, water and solute

common_misconceptions:
  - Homeostasis means conditions never change (it means conditions are kept within a narrow functional range, with continuous adjustment).
  - Only warm-blooded animals maintain homeostasis (all living organisms regulate some internal conditions).
  - Homeostasis is a static set point rather than dynamic balance (it is a dynamic equilibrium maintained by negative feedback).

## Notes

Homeostasis connects cell biology to macroscopic physiology: the same negative-feedback
logic that buffers temperature also underpins enzyme regulation and organism survival.
