name: Chemical Equilibrium
domain: chemistry
status: draft
definition: >-
  The state of a reversible reaction in which the rates of the forward and reverse reactions are
  equal, so the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant over time. Equilibrium is
  dynamic and does not imply equal quantities of reactants and products.
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "The Haber process N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ establishes equilibrium at high pressure."
  - "Dissolving silver chloride, AgCl(s) ⇌ Ag⁺ + Cl⁻, reaches a solubility equilibrium."
key_experiments:
  - "Equilibrate iodine and iodide between aqueous and organic phases and measure the partition ratio."
common_misconceptions:
  - At equilibrium the reaction has stopped (forward and reverse reactions continue at equal rates).
  - Equilibrium means equal amounts of reactants and products (concentrations are constant, not necessarily equal).
learning_objectives:
  - Describe the dynamic nature of chemical equilibrium.
  - Write the equilibrium position for a reversible reaction.
  - Explain how changes in conditions disturb and re-establish equilibrium.
real_world_applications:
  - Industrial ammonia synthesis and the Contact process for sulfuric acid.
  - Gas exchange in blood (oxygen and carbon dioxide equilibria).
