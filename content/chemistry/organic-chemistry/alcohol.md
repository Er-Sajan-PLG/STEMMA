definition: >-
  An organic compound containing one or more hydroxyl (—OH) functional groups bonded to a saturated
  carbon atom. Alcohols are polar, hydrogen-bonding liquids that range from methanol and ethanol to
  high-molecular-mass polyols.

  - "React an alcohol with sodium metal to observe hydrogen evolution, confirming the hydroxyl group."
common_misconceptions:
  - Alcohols and the hydroxyl group make a solution basic (alcohols are generally neutral or weakly acidic).
  - All alcohols are safe to consume (methanol is toxic).
learning_objectives:
  - Name simple alcohols and describe hydrogen bonding among them.
  - Explain oxidation of primary and secondary alcohols.
  - Compare boiling points of alcohols with analogous alkanes.

  - Ethanol as a biofuel and solvent; methanol in fuels and synthesis.
  - Isopropyl alcohol as a disinfectant.
