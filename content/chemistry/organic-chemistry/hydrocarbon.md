name: Hydrocarbon

definition: >-
  An organic molecule composed entirely of carbon and hydrogen atoms. Hydrocarbons are the
  fundamental framework of organic chemistry, classified into alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, and
  aromatic compounds according to the bonding between carbon atoms.

examples:
  - "Methane (CH₄) and octane (C₈H₁₈, a component of gasoline) are saturated alkanes."
  - "Ethene (C₂H₄) and benzene (C₆H₆) contain multiple carbon-carbon bonds."
key_experiments:
  - "Burn a hydrocarbon in air and test the gases with limewater to show carbon dioxide forms."
common_misconceptions:
  - All hydrocarbons are liquids (small hydrocarbons such as methane are gases at room temperature).
  - Hydrocarbons dissolve in water (they are non-polar and largely immiscible with water).
learning_objectives:
  - Classify hydrocarbons by the types of carbon-carbon bonds present.
  - Explain why carbon forms the backbone of so many distinct compounds.
  - Distinguish saturated from unsaturated hydrocarbons.
real_world_applications:
  - Fuels (natural gas, petrol, diesel) and lubricants derived from crude oil.
  - Feedstocks for plastics, solvents, and synthetic materials.
