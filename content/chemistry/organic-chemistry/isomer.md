name: Isomer

definition: >-
  One of two or more compounds that have the same molecular formula but different arrangements of
  atoms, and therefore different structures or spatial configurations. Isomerism may be structural
  (constitutional) or stereochemical.
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "Butane and 2-methylpropane share the formula C₄H₁₀ but differ in carbon skeleton."
  - "Cis- and trans-2-butene are geometric isomers differing in bond orientation."
key_experiments:
  - "Build molecular models of structural isomers to compare their connectivity and shapes."
common_misconceptions:
  - Same formula always means the same compound (isomers share a formula but differ in structure).
  - Isomers have identical physical and chemical properties (different structure changes properties).
learning_objectives:
  - Distinguish constitutional and stereoisomerism.
  - Count and draw isomers for a given molecular formula.
  - Explain the role of isomerism in the diversity of organic compounds.
real_world_applications:
  - Drug enantiomers can differ in pharmacological effect (e.g. thalidomide).
  - Octane isomers differ in fuel performance.
