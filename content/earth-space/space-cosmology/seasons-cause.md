common_misconceptions:
  - Seasons are caused by Earth being significantly closer to the Sun in summer (Earth is actually closest to the Sun in January during Northern Hemisphere winter).
  - Both Northern and Southern hemispheres experience summer at the same time (hemispheres experience opposite seasons due to axial tilt orientation).

## Notes

Earth's axial tilt causes changes in solar intensity per unit surface area and daylight duration across the year.
