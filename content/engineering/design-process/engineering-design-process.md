common_misconceptions:
  - Engineering design is a linear step-by-step procedure (it is iterative with multiple feedback loops).
  - Engineering design is the same as scientific inquiry (science seeks to explain natural phenomena; engineering solves practical human problems under constraints).
learning_objectives:
  - Describe the iterative steps of the engineering design process.
  - Distinguish design criteria (requirements) from design constraints (limitations).
  - Evaluate trade-offs between competing design criteria (e.g. cost vs safety vs performance).

## Notes

Engineering applies scientific and mathematical principles to design solutions meeting human needs within constraints.
