name: Algebraic Expression
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  A combination of variables, numbers, and arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction,
  multiplication, division, exponentiation) that represents a value. Unlike an equation,
  an expression has no equality sign and cannot be "solved" — it can only be simplified
  or evaluated.
symbol: null

examples:
  - "3x + 5"
  - "2x² - 7x + 1"
  - "(x + 2)(x - 3)"
  - "√(x² + y²)"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Expressions can be solved (they can only be simplified or evaluated)"
  - "3x means 3 × x but 3x² means (3x)² (it means 3 × x²)"
  - "x + x = x² (it equals 2x)"
learning_objectives:
  - Identify terms, coefficients, constants, and like terms
  - Simplify expressions by combining like terms
  - Expand and factor expressions
  - Evaluate expressions for given variable values
  - Apply the distributive property correctly
real_world_applications:
  - Writing formulas for area, volume, perimeter
  - Cost/revenue/profit models in business
  - Physics equations (kinetic energy = ½mv²)
