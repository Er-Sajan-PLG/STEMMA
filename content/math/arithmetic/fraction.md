name: Fraction
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  A numerical quantity that is not a whole number, representing a part of a whole.
  Written as a/b where a is the numerator (number of parts) and b ≠ 0 is the denominator
  (total equal parts in the whole).
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "3/4 means 3 parts out of 4 equal parts"
  - "5/2 is an improper fraction (numerator > denominator)"
  - "1 1/2 is a mixed number (whole + fraction)"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Larger denominator means larger fraction (1/8 < 1/4 despite 8 > 4)"
  - "You cannot add fractions with different denominators (you can, by finding a common denominator)"
  - "Multiplying fractions always makes them smaller (multiplying by a fraction > 1 makes it larger)"
learning_objectives:
  - Identify numerator and denominator
  - Simplify fractions to lowest terms
  - Convert between improper fractions and mixed numbers
  - Add, subtract, multiply, and divide fractions
  - Compare and order fractions
real_world_applications:
  - Recipes and cooking measurements
  - Construction and carpentry (measuring tape fractions)
  - Probability (favorable outcomes / total outcomes)
key_experiments:
  - "Fraction strips or circles: physically combine and compare fractions"
