name: Ratio
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  A comparison of two quantities by division, expressed as a:b or a/b. Ratios
  describe the relative size of two quantities without requiring a common unit
  (unlike percentages which are always per hundred).
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "Classroom with 12 boys and 18 girls: ratio 12:18 = 2:3"
  - "Map scale 1:50000 means 1 unit on map = 50000 units in reality"
  - "Recipe ratio: 2 cups flour : 1 cup sugar"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Ratios and fractions are the same (3:2 means 3/5 and 2/5 of total, not 3/2)"
  - "Ratio order doesn't matter (2:3 ≠ 3:2)"
  - "Ratios must be whole numbers (can be decimals: 1.5:1)"
learning_objectives:
  - Write and simplify ratios
  - Divide a quantity in a given ratio
  - Solve problems involving direct proportion using ratios
  - Apply ratios to scale drawings, maps, and models
real_world_applications:
  - Scale drawings and maps
  - Mixing proportions (paint, concrete, recipes)
  - Financial ratios (debt-to-equity, current ratio)
  - Gear ratios in mechanics
key_experiments:
  - "Scale model building: measure and scale down real objects"
