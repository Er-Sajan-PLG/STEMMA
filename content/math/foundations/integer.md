name: Integer
domain: mathematics

definition: >-
  The set of whole numbers and their negatives, including zero: ..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...
  Integers extend natural numbers to include negative values and zero, enabling subtraction
  to be closed within the set.
symbol: ℤ
unit: null
equation: null
examples:
  - "Temperature: -10°C, 0°C, 25°C"
  - "Bank balance: -$50 (overdraft), $0, $100"
  - "Elevation: -400m (Dead Sea), 0m (sea level), 8848m (Everest)"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Integers are only negative numbers (they include positives and zero)"
  - "Zero is positive or negative (zero is neither)"
  - "Subtracting a negative number always gives a smaller number (subtracting a negative adds its absolute value)"
learning_objectives:
  - Identify integers and represent them on a number line
  - Perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of integers
  - Understand absolute value as distance from zero
  - Apply integer operations to real-world contexts (temperature, finance, elevation)
real_world_applications:
  - Temperature scales (Celsius, Fahrenheit)
  - Financial accounting (credits and debits)
  - Elevation and depth measurements
  - Coordinate systems
