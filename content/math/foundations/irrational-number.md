definition: >-
  A real number that cannot be expressed as a ratio of two integers. Its decimal
  representation neither terminates nor repeats. Together with rational numbers, they
  form the real numbers.

common_misconceptions:
  - "Irrational means 'illogical' or 'unreasonable' (it means 'not a ratio')"
  - "All square roots are irrational (√4 = 2 is rational; only square roots of non-perfect squares are irrational)"
  - "Irrational numbers cannot be approximated (they can be approximated to any desired precision)"
learning_objectives:
  - Recognize that irrational numbers cannot be written as fractions
  - Identify common irrational numbers (π, √2, √3, e)
  - Approximate irrational numbers using rational numbers
  - Understand that the sum/product of a rational and irrational is irrational (if rational ≠ 0)
