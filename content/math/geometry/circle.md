id: stemma:math.circle
type: concept
name: Circle
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The set of all points in a plane that are at a fixed distance (radius) from a fixed point (center).
  Key elements: radius (r), diameter (d = 2r), circumference (C), area (A).
symbol: ⭘
unit: null
equation: C = 2πr = πd  |  A = πr²
examples:
  - "Wheel: radius = 30 cm → circumference ≈ 188.5 cm"
  - "Pizza: diameter = 12 inches → area ≈ 113.1 sq in"
  - "Unit circle: r = 1, C = 2π, A = π"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Circumference and area formulas are the same (C = 2πr, A = πr²)"
  - "Diameter is half the radius (d = 2r, so radius is half the diameter)"
  - "π = 3.14 exactly (π is irrational; 3.14 is an approximation)"
learning_objectives:
  - Identify radius, diameter, chord, tangent, secant, arc, sector
  - Calculate circumference and area
  - Understand π as the ratio C/d
  - Solve problems involving arcs and sectors
  - Apply circle theorems (angle at center = 2 × angle at circumference, etc.)
real_world_applications:
  - Wheels and gears
  - Circular motion (centripetal acceleration)
  - Architecture (domes, arches)
  - Astronomy (orbits)
key_experiments:
  - "Measure circumference/diameter of circular objects to approximate π"
