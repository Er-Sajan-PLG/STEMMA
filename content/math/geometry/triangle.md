name: Triangle
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  A polygon with three edges and three vertices. The sum of interior angles is always 180°.
  Triangles are classified by sides (equilateral, isosceles, scalene) and angles (acute, right, obtuse).
symbol: △
unit: null
equation: ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°
examples:
  - "Right triangle: 3-4-5 sides, 90° angle"
  - "Equilateral triangle: all sides equal, all angles 60°"
  - "Isosceles triangle: two equal sides, two equal angles"
common_misconceptions:
  - "All triangles have a right angle (only right triangles do)"
  - "The largest angle is always opposite the longest side (this is TRUE)"
  - "Two triangles with same angles are congruent (they are similar, not necessarily congruent)"
learning_objectives:
  - Classify triangles by sides and angles
  - Apply angle sum property (180°)
  - Use Pythagorean theorem for right triangles
  - Apply triangle inequality (sum of any two sides > third side)
  - Identify congruent (SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS) and similar triangles
real_world_applications:
  - Trigonometry (solving triangles)
  - Structural engineering (trusses)
  - Navigation (triangulation)
  - Computer graphics (triangulation)
key_experiments:
  - "Triangle construction: given 3 sides/angles, can you build it?"
