id: stemma:math.mean
type: quantity
name: Mean (Arithmetic Average)
domain: mathematics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The sum of all values in a data set divided by the number of values. It represents
  the central tendency or "balance point" of the data.
symbol: x̄, μ
unit: same as data units
equation: x̄ = (∑xᵢ) / n
examples:
  - "Test scores: 80, 85, 90, 95 → mean = 350/4 = 87.5"
  - "Daily temperatures over a week: sum of 7 values / 7"
common_misconceptions:
  - "Mean is always one of the data values (it may not be)"
  - "Mean is unaffected by outliers (it is highly sensitive to extreme values)"
  - "Mean, median, and mode are the same (they differ for skewed distributions)"
learning_objectives:
  - Calculate mean for ungrouped and grouped data
  - Understand effect of outliers on mean
  - Compare mean with median and mode
  - Interpret mean in context
real_world_applications:
  - Grade point average (GPA)
  - Average income, temperature, speed
  - Quality control (process mean)
key_experiments:
  - "Balance point: find where a ruler balances with weights at data positions"
