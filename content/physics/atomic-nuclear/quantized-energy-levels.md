name: Quantized Energy Levels

definition: The discrete set of energies that a bound system, such as an atom, may possess.
  Transitions between levels involve the absorption or emission of a photon whose energy equals
  the difference between the two levels.

equation: ΔE = E_final - E_initial = h·f

  - "Absorption spectra show dark lines at wavelengths corresponding to allowed upward transitions."
key_experiments:
  - "The Franck–Hertz experiment demonstrates that electrons deposit energy into atoms only in discrete steps."
  - "Spectroscopy of a gas reveals its characteristic quantized level structure."
common_misconceptions:
  - "An atom can have only discrete energies; it cannot occupy arbitrary intermediate energies."
  - "The emitted photon's energy equals the level difference, not the level's absolute energy."
