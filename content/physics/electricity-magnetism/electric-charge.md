type: quantity
name: Electric Charge
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: A fundamental property of matter that causes it to experience a force
  when placed in an electromagnetic field. Charge can be positive or negative; like
  charges repel, opposite charges attract.
symbol: Q or q
unit: coulomb (C)
equation: null
examples:
- A balloon rubbed on hair attracts small paper pieces.
- Lightning is a large flow of charge between cloud and ground.
key_experiments:
- 'Electrostatics: charge a balloon or rod by rubbing and detect it with a pith-ball
  or electroscope.'
- Charging a rod by friction and attracting small pieces of paper.
common_misconceptions:
- Charge is created by friction (charge is transferred, not created).
- A charged object always attracts uncharged objects (a charged object can attract
  a neutral one through polarization).
- Electrons are the only charge carriers (ions can also carry charge).
learning_objectives:
- Describe electric charge as a fundamental property.
- Explain charging by friction, conduction, and induction.
- State Coulomb's law and calculate electrostatic force.
- Distinguish between conductors and insulators.
real_world_applications:
- Static electricity (lightning, photocopiers).
- Capacitors.
- Electrostatic painting.
