name: Electromagnetism

definition: The branch of physics dealing with the relationship between electricity
  and magnetism. A changing electric field produces a magnetic field, and a changing
  magnetic field produces an electric field.
symbol: null

equation: null
examples:
- A scrapyard electromagnet lifts cars when current flows.
- Electric doorbells and relays use electromagnets.
key_experiments:
- 'Wrap an iron nail in insulated wire to a cell: it picks up paperclips, and drops
  them when the current stops.'
- Oersted's experiment (compass needle deflects near a current-carrying wire).
common_misconceptions:
- Electricity and magnetism are separate phenomena (they are two aspects of the same
  force).
- Only magnets produce magnetic fields (current-carrying wires also produce them).
learning_objectives:
- Explain that a current-carrying conductor produces a magnetic field.
- Describe the magnetic effect of current (Oersted's experiment).
- Explain the force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field.
- 'Describe applications: electric motor, loudspeaker.'
real_world_applications:
- Electric motors.

- Electromagnets (scrap yards, relays).
