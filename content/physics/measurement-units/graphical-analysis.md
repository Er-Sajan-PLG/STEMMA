common_misconceptions:
  - The slope of a distance-time graph is acceleration (it is velocity).
  - The area under a velocity-time graph is distance (it is displacement).
  - Curved graphs always mean acceleration (depends on the graph type).

  - Plot and interpret distance-time graphs.
  - Plot and interpret velocity-time graphs.
  - Calculate speed/velocity from the slope of a distance-time graph.
  - Calculate acceleration from the slope of a velocity-time graph.
  - Calculate displacement from the area under a velocity-time graph.
