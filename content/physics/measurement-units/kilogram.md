The kilogram (symbol: kg) is the SI base unit of mass. It is defined by taking the fixed numerical value of the Planck constant h to be exactly 6.62607015e-34 when expressed in the unit joule second, J s, which is equal to kg m2 s-1, where the metre and the second are defined in terms of c and ΔνCs. Exact per BIPM definition of the kilogram (SI Brochure 9th ed., 2019, §2.3.1.2; the text of the 26th CGPM 2018, Resolution 1).
