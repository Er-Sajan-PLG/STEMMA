Mass is the fundamental physical quantity measuring inertia and amount of substance of a body. It is one of the seven base quantities of the SI (dimension M); its SI base unit is the kilogram (kg), defined by taking the fixed numerical exact value of the Planck constant h = 6.62607015e-34 J s. Agreed per BIPM SI Brochure 9th ed. (2019).
