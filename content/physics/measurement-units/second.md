The second (symbol: s) is the SI base unit of time. It is defined by taking the fixed numerical value of the caesium frequency ΔνCs, the unperturbed ground-state hyperfine transition frequency of the caesium-133 atom, to be exactly 9,192,631,770 when expressed in the unit hertz, which is equal to s-1. Exact per BIPM definition of the second (SI Brochure 9th ed., 2019, §2.3.1.1; the text of the 26th CGPM 2018, Resolution 1).
