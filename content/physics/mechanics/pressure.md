type: quantity
name: Pressure
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The force applied per unit area on a surface. Pressure explains how forces are distributed
  over areas.
symbol: P
unit: pascal (Pa)
equation: P = F / A
examples:
  - "A drawing pin concentrates force onto a tiny point."
  - "Tyres and balloons hold air at pressure above the atmosphere."
common_misconceptions:
  - Pressure and force are the same (force is total; pressure is force per unit area).
  - Pressure always acts downward (pressure acts equally in all directions in a fluid).
  - Larger area means more pressure (larger area means less pressure for the same force).
learning_objectives:
  - Define pressure as force per unit area.
  - Calculate pressure from force and area.
  - Explain everyday examples of pressure (knives, shoes, hydraulic systems).
  - Distinguish between pressure, force, and area.
real_world_applications:
  - Hydraulic systems (brakes, jacks).
  - Knives and cutting tools (small area = high pressure).
  - Snowshoes and camel feet (large area = low pressure).
  - Atmospheric pressure.
key_experiments:
  - "Pressure in fluids increases with depth."
