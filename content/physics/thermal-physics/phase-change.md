name: Phase Change
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: A transition of matter between solid, liquid, and gas states in which the identity
  of the substance is preserved while its internal structure changes. Latent heat is absorbed
  or released at constant temperature during such a transition.
symbol: L
unit: joule per kilogram (J/kg) for latent heat
equation: Q = m·L
examples:
  - "Melting ice absorbs latent heat of fusion without raising its temperature."
  - "Boiling water releases latent heat of vaporization when condensing back to liquid."
key_experiments:
  - "Heat ice-water and record a flat temperature plateau during melting despite continuous heat input."
common_misconceptions:
  - "During a phase change at constant pressure, temperature stays fixed while heat is absorbed or released."
  - "Latent heat is not a temperature rise; it is energy used to change the state of matter."
learning_objectives:
  - "Use latent heat to compute heat absorbed or released during a phase change."
  - "Interpret a heating curve showing plateaus at phase-change temperatures."
real_world_applications:
  - "Sweating and evaporation cool the body via latent heat."
  - "Refrigeration and air conditioning exploit latent heat of vaporization."
