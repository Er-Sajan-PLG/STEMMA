name: Lens
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: A transparent optical device that refracts light to converge or diverge
  rays. Convex (converging) lenses bring light to a focus; concave (diverging) lenses
  spread light apart.
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u (lens formula)
examples:
- A magnifying glass focuses sunlight to a burning point.
- A camera lens forms a real, inverted image on a screen or sensor.
key_experiments:
- 'Convex lens: form a real image of a distant object on a screen and measure the
  focal length.'
- Finding the focal length of a convex lens.
- Drawing ray diagrams for different object positions.
common_misconceptions:
- All lenses magnify (concave lenses reduce image size).
- Lenses work by reflection (they work by refraction).
- Thicker lenses always have shorter focal length (depends on curvature and refractive
  index).
learning_objectives:
- Distinguish between convex and concave lenses.
- Draw ray diagrams for image formation by lenses.
- Use the lens formula to calculate image position.
- Explain how lenses are used in optical instruments.
real_world_applications:
- Eyeglasses and contact lenses.
- Cameras.
- Microscopes and telescopes.
- Magnifying glasses.
